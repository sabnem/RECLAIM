Privacy Policy

RECLAIM (the "Service") helps people report, find, and return lost items. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: name, email address, profile photo, and any information you add to your profile when you register.
  • Item Reports: item titles, descriptions, photos, location details, and any other information you provide when reporting an item.
  • Communications: messages exchanged between users (owner and finder) including message text and images you attach.
  • Usage Data: analytics, IP address, device and browser information, and cookies used to operate and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Service (match owners and finders, display items, deliver messages and notifications).
  • To communicate with you about item returns, account activity, and support requests.
  • To improve the Service, detect and prevent fraud or abuse, and for analytics and research.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

  • Other Users: When you report an item or send a message, relevant information (item details, photos, your display name) is shared with the other user involved in the return process.
  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted third-party providers (email delivery, hosting, analytics) who perform services on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requests: We may disclose information to comply with laws, respond to legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of RECLAIM, our users, or others.
  • Aggregated Data: Non-identifying, aggregated statistics may be shared publicly or with partners.

4. Photos & Media

Photos you upload for item reports or in messages are stored so the Service can function. Only users involved in a report or conversation can access those images, unless otherwise required by law. Please avoid uploading sensitive personal information in photos.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account information, item reports, and messages as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. If you request account deletion, we will remove or anonymize personal information subject to backup retention limitations and legal requirements.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. However, no system is completely secure—please exercise care when sharing personal information on the platform.

7. Your Choices

  • Account Settings: You can update your profile information and email preferences from your account page.
  • Access & Deletion: You may request a copy of your personal data or request deletion by contacting us (see contact section). Some information may remain in backups or if required for legal reasons.
  • Cookies: You can control cookies via your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect Service functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if we learn we have, we will take steps to delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We will post the revised policy with a new effective date. Continued use after changes indicates acceptance.

10. Contact

If you have questions or requests about your data, contact us at privacy@reclaim.example. Replace this address with your project administrator email in production.
